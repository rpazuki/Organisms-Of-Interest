# Ranking rubric (draft — weights to be agreed before scoring)

Organisms are scored on four axes, each 0-10, then weighted:

1. Literature depth — publication count and recency for metabolic/GEM work.
2. Model completeness — gene/reaction/metabolite coverage vs. genome size; compartmentalisation.
3. GEM quality — MEMOTE score, mass/charge balance, SBML level, presence of a consensus model.
4. Recency — year of the most recent published SBML release.

Scores, weights, and the resulting order go to `02_comparative/tables/ranking.csv`.
Every score must cite its source; unsourced scores are not entered.
